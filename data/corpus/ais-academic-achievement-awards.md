# Academic Achievement Awards

## Undergraduate and graduate awards

High academic achievement in diploma, bachelor and graduate diploma studies is
recognised by the award of certificates of "Merit" (minimum GPA of 6.5) and
"Excellence" (GPA of 7.5 and above). The GPA is calculated on all course
grades achieved for the current programme in the prior one to two trimesters,
totalling not less than 60 credits, and not previously counted for an award.

## Postgraduate awards

A "Certificate of Academic Excellence in Postgraduate Studies" is awarded for
a GPA of 6.5 or above for the first block of 60 credits (or 90 Level 8 credits
for the Master of Information Technology). A "Certificate of Sustained
Academic Excellence in Postgraduate Studies" is awarded for a GPA of 6.5 or
above for a subsequent block of 60 to 90 credits.

## Award ceremony

A Recognition of Academic Achievement ceremony is held each trimester for the
presentation of awards. Full details of the awards are in the Student
Handbook.

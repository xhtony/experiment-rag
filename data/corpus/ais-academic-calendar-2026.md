# Academic Calendar and Trimesters 2026

## Trimester dates 2026 - Business, Hospitality Management, IT and NZCEL

For Business, Hospitality Management, Information Technology and NZCEL
programmes, the 2026 trimester dates are: Trimester 1, 2026 runs from
2 February to 8 May; Trimester 2, 2026 runs from 25 May to 28 August; and
Trimester 3, 2026 runs from 14 September to 18 December. Start dates in 2026
are 2 February, 25 May and 14 September.

## Schedule 2026 - PGCBA, PGDBA and MBA

For the Postgraduate Certificate in Business Administration, Postgraduate
Diploma in Business Administration and Master of Business Administration,
start dates in 2026 are 31 January, 28 March, 23 May, 18 July, 12 September
and 31 October. The MBA and PGDBA classes run every weekend from 31 January
2026 until 18 December 2026.

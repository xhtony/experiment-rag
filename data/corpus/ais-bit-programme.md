# Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT) Programme

## Programme details

The Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT) at Auckland Institute of Studies
(AIS) is a Level 7 qualification of 360 credits, with a typical duration of
36 months (7-9 semesters full-time). The BIT prepares students to become IT
professionals with a breadth of knowledge across a range of IT sub-disciplines
and a depth of understanding in a specialisation, allowing them to lead and
become strong participants in IT team projects. All BIT students are issued
with their own laptop and receive the necessary free Microsoft software.

## Programme structure

The BIT comprises 23 courses, including compulsory and elective courses from:
Networks and Security, Information Systems and Software Development. The
courses are delivered in three stages over three years: Stage 1 consists of
Level 5 courses, Stage 2 of Level 6 courses, and Stage 3 of Level 7 courses.
Each course is 15 credits, except for the Information Technology Project,
which is 30 credits and is completed over two semesters.

## Compulsory courses

The compulsory courses of the BIT are: COMP510 Fundamentals of Information
Systems and Technology, COMP511 Information Technology Professional Skills,
COMP513 Fundamentals of Computer Programming, COMP514 Fundamentals of Computer
Databases, COMP515 Fundamentals of Networks and Security, BUSS502 Business
Environment, MATH501 Essentials of Mathematics and Statistics, COMP602
Computer System Testing, COMP609 Information System Security, COMP617
Requirements Modelling, COMP621 Operating Systems, COMP701 Information
Technology Project Management, and COMP720 Information Technology Project
(completed over two semesters).

## Software Development specialisation

The Software Development specialisation equips students with the skills to
analyse, design, develop, test and deploy business software solutions.
Software development is taught using the Python, Java and C# languages, in the
.NET and Android environments. The specialisation courses are: COMP601 Systems
Analysis and Design, SOFT605 Object Oriented Programming, SOFT606 Desktop
Applications Development, SOFT703 Web Applications Development, SOFT704
Human-Computer Interaction, and SOFT708 Mobile Applications Development.

## Computer Networks specialisation

The Networks and Security specialisation equips students with skills in
computer network engineering, server systems and network security. Students
should verify the availability of this specialisation with the Market Manager.
The specialisation courses are: CONE618 Server Administration, CONE622
Intermediate Computer Networking, CONE623 Cloud Computing, CONE709 Network
System Security, CONE710 Advanced Networks and Security, and CONE711 Wireless
Network Design.

## Information Systems specialisation

The Information Systems specialisation prepares students for the data-driven
industry by equipping them with the analytical skills to develop and support
organisations' IT and enterprise systems. Students should verify the
availability of this specialisation with the Market Manager. The
specialisation courses are: COMP601 Systems Analysis and Design, SOFT605
Object Oriented Programming, INFO620 Enterprise Resource Planning Systems,
INFO712 Management Information Systems, INFO714 E-Business Strategies, and
INFO716 Business Intelligence and Analytics.

## Electives

Electives may be chosen from the courses available for the specialisations as
well as the following: COMP609 Information Systems Security, INFO614
Intermediate Computer Databases, COMP722 Information Technology Industry
Practice, COMP512 Fundamentals of Technical Support, COMP516 Fundamentals of
Network Security, COMP517 Hardware and Software Service Provisioning, and
COMP518 Information Technology Systems Maintenance and Service Management.
The inclusion of courses outside the Information Technology programme may be
considered with the approval of the Academic Head of Programme.

## Entry requirements

Entry to the BIT requires one of the following: a minimum of 14 NCEA credits
in each of 3 subjects at Level 3 including 9 credits in mathematics, and a
minimum of 14 numeracy credits at Level 1 or higher, and a minimum of 8
literacy credits at Level 2 or higher; or successful tertiary study, including
mathematics at a level equivalent to 9 credits at Level 3; or successful
completion of a Level 4 qualification in Computing; or an equivalent overseas
qualification. In addition, applicants require an English level of IELTS 6.0
with no individual band lower than 5.5 / TOEFL iBT 60 (with a writing score of
18) / PToE academic score of 50 with no band lower than 42 / NZCEL (Academic)
Level 4, and must be 17 years or over.

## Fees

For international students, the 2025 programme fees are NZ$21,304 per 120
credits plus resource fees of NZ$2,280, a total of NZ$23,584 per 120 credits.
For domestic students, the 2025 programme fees are NZ$4,216 per 120 credits
plus resource fees of NZ$2,280, a total of NZ$6,496 per 120 credits. Both are
subject to the compulsory Student Services Fee of NZ$300 per year (or NZ$30
per month or part thereof). The programme is eligible for a student loan from
StudyLink.

## Career outcomes

The BIT is designed for a range of careers depending on the specialisation
selected. Software Development: software programmer, software developer,
software analyst, software designer. Computer Networks: network engineer,
network administrator, network support specialist. Information Systems:
junior project manager, IT manager, business analyst.

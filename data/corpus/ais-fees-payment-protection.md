# Fees, Payment and Fees Protection

## Fees protection trust account

All student fees are deposited directly into an independent Chartered
Accountant's Trust Account and are released to AIS in the following manner.
Tuition fees: the lesser of 20% or $3,000 is released to AIS after the
statutory withdrawal period after commencement of the programme, and the
remaining amount periodically in arrears on a pro rata basis in relation to
the tuition delivered. Accommodation or living costs are released periodically
in arrears on a pro rata basis. The trust account is managed by an independent
Chartered Accountant firm.

## Payment obligations and penalties

Students who are not up to date with payments of fees will not be entitled to
continue the qualification or to graduate from it. If payment is overdue, AIS
will charge penalty interest on any overdue amount at the rate of 2.5% per
month from the overdue date until payment in full is received by AIS. The
student will upon demand pay all AIS expenses and legal costs in the
collection of all overdue fees, plus debt collection agency costs.

## Application procedure - international students

Upon receipt of an Offer of Place, international students may undergo
Pre-Visa Assessment by applying for a visa, and should receive Approval In
Principle prior to paying tuition fees. Alternatively, transfer full payment
to the AIS Student Trust account. All fees are converted to New Zealand
Dollars at the time of receipt. After payment is processed, the student is
sent a Receipt for fees paid and a Confirmation of Enrolment document. The
Receipt and Confirmation of Enrolment must be sent when applying for the
Student Visa. Students requiring airport pick-up or accommodation must inform
AIS at least two weeks before arrival in New Zealand.

## Application procedure - domestic students

Domestic students transfer payment of fees to the AIS Student Trust account
prior to the commencement of the programme. Students funding their course
through a Student Loan should finalise the Student Loan application with
StudyLink and arrange for any balance of fees to be paid to AIS prior to
commencement of studies. Successful applicants will be sent an Invoice, and a
Receipt will be issued when payment is made.

## Bring Your Own Device requirements

AIS uses e-books and other electronic methods to deliver quality education in
a BYOD environment. A student's own device should meet the following minimum
standards: an iOS 13, Android 12 or MS Windows 11 operating system in English,
compatible with Office 365, at least a 10-inch screen or larger, memory 8GB
(or preferably 16GB), Wi-Fi capability, and with mouse, keyboard or
touchscreen, webcam, microphone and speakers.

## Fee reviews

For programmes extending across more than one year, the institute reserves the
right to review its fees annually and students are advised to make allowance
for modest annual increases in line with general cost increases. AIS also
reserves the right to adjust fees as required in response to changes by
external suppliers. Students are liable for any exchange rate differential
between the time of payment and receipt if payment is not made in NZD.

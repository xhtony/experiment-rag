# Graduate Diploma in Information Technology (GDIT) Programme

## Programme details

The Graduate Diploma in Information Technology (GDIT) at Auckland Institute of
Studies (AIS) is a Level 7 qualification of 135 credits, with a typical
duration of 12 months (3 semesters full-time). The GDIT meets the needs of
students who have completed a bachelor's degree in a non-IT field and wish to
develop their IT skills, and also assists industry professionals to gain the
discipline and recognition of a formal qualification.

## Programme structure

The GDIT comprises 8 courses chosen from one of three specialist topics:
Software Development, Computer Networks, and Information Systems. Seven
courses are 15 credits each, plus an Information Technology Project or an
Information Technology Internship of 30 credits (two course equivalents).

## Software Development specialist topic

The Software Development specialist topic comprises: COMP504 Fundamentals of
Computer Databases, SOFT605 Object Oriented Programming, SOFT606 Desktop
Applications Development, COMP617 Requirements Modelling, COMP701 Information
Technology Project Management, SOFT703 Web Applications Development, SOFT708
Mobile Applications Development, and COMP721 Intensive Information Technology
Project.

## Computer Networks specialist topic

The Computer Networks specialist topic comprises: COMP505 Fundamentals of
Computer Networking, COMP617 Requirements Modelling, CONE618 Server
Administration, CONE622 Intermediate Computer Networking, COMP701 Information
Technology Project Management, CONE709 Network System Security, CONE710
Advanced Computer Networking, and COMP721 Intensive Information Technology
Project.

## Information Systems specialist topic

The Information Systems specialist topic comprises: COMP504 Fundamentals of
Computer Databases, COMP601 Systems Analysis and Design, COMP617 Requirements
Modelling, INFO620 Enterprise Resource Planning Systems, COMP701 Information
Technology Project Management, INFO712 Management Information Systems, INFO714
E-Business Strategies, and COMP721 Intensive Information Technology Project.

## Entry requirements

Entry to the GDIT requires a bachelor degree at least equivalent to New
Zealand pass standard, and an English level of IELTS 6.0 with no individual
band lower than 5.5 / TOEFL iBT 60 (with a writing score of 18) / PToE
academic score of 50 with no band lower than 42 / NZCEL (Academic) Level 4.
Students with a lesser qualification plus work experience may be considered.

## Fees

For international students, the 2025 programme fees are NZ$23,967 plus
resource fees of NZ$2,565, a total of NZ$26,532. For domestic students, the
2025 programme fees are NZ$4,725 plus resource fees of NZ$2,565, a total of
NZ$7,290. Both are subject to the compulsory Student Services Fee of NZ$300
per year (or NZ$30 per month or part thereof).

## Start dates

The GDIT has start dates aligned with the AIS trimester system, with intakes
in February and September (2025 start dates: 3 February and 15 September).

# Graduation Ceremony 2026

## Ceremony details

The 2026 AIS graduation ceremony will be held at the New Zealand International
Convention Centre in Auckland on Monday 28 September. It is a daytime
ceremony. All eligible graduates are encouraged to take the opportunity to
formally graduate and participate in this event.

## Eligibility

All students who successfully complete a qualification at Level 5 or above
between October 2025 and early September 2026 inclusive are eligible to attend
the 2026 ceremony.

## Academic dress

Appropriate academic dress is worn at the ceremony: gown, hood and trencher
for degree graduates; gown and sash for diploma graduates; and a gown for
certificate graduates. The wearing of national dress is encouraged.

## Application deadline

Applications to attend the 2026 graduation ceremony, together with payment of
the academic dress hireage fee, must be submitted by 5.00pm on Friday
11 September.

## Requirements to formally graduate

To formally graduate at the ceremony, a student must: have completed their
qualification(s) within the eligibility period; have paid all outstanding fees
and/or fines and completed a Student Clearance Form; have completed and
submitted an Application to Graduate form before the specified deadline; and
have returned their certificate to Registry if it has already been issued.

## Applying before final results

Students who expect to complete their programme close to the ceremony should
not wait for final results before applying. It is best to complete and submit
an Application to Graduate form as early as possible, because applications are
not accepted after the submission deadline. If a student fails to complete
their qualification in time for the ceremony, the application is withdrawn and
any graduation fees paid are refunded.

## Graduation programme

The programme printed for the graduation ceremony includes the names of all
students who completed qualifications within the eligibility period, as well
as those who achieved certificates of proficiency in the AIS English Language
Centre.

## Invitation letters for overseas guests

Family members overseas who wish to attend the ceremony can be supported with
their temporary visitor visa applications. Registry can prepare an invitation
letter to support the required visa application; students complete a Request
for Invitation Letter form and submit it to Registry.

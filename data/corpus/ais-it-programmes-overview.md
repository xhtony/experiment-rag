# Information Technology Programmes at AIS Overview

## IT at AIS

Computing and IT skills are essential in almost all career paths. In New
Zealand, several occupations within the IT category are on Immigration New
Zealand's Essential Skills lists, and many are also specified on the Long Term
Skill Shortage list. On average, there are between 2,000 and 3,000 roles in
Information Technology regularly advertised in New Zealand. AIS IT programmes
incorporate both hands-on training and theoretical knowledge, are delivered by
staff with top-level academic qualifications and industry experience, and
combine regular industry visits with classroom teaching. All AIS IT students
are issued with their own laptop and receive free Microsoft software.

## IT programme ladder

AIS offers the following Information Technology programmes: the New Zealand
Diploma in Information Technology Technical Support (Level 5), the Bachelor of
Information Technology (Level 7), the Graduate Diploma in Information
Technology (Level 7), the Postgraduate Diploma in Information Technology
(Level 8), and the Master of Information Technology (Level 9).

## About AIS

Established in 1990, Auckland Institute of Studies is one of New Zealand's
largest independent degree-granting institutions and an NZQA Category 1
provider. AIS offers a broad range of NZQA-approved programmes from beginner's
English through to master's degree qualifications, including diploma and
degree programmes in business, hospitality management, information technology
and English language. The flexible three-trimester system and cross-credit
recognition allow students to fast-track their studies. The AIS campus is
located at 28a Linwood Avenue, Mt Albert, Auckland.

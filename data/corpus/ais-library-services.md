# AIS Library Services and Facilities

The AIS Library is located at St Helens Campus and provides resources,
expertise and facilities to support learning, teaching and research.

## Opening hours

The Library is open Monday to Friday from 9.00am to 5.00pm, and on Saturday
and Sunday from 11.00am to 3.00pm. The Library is closed on public holidays.

## Facilities and collections

Library facilities and collections include: books, periodicals, DVDs, CDs,
audiobooks and audio players; a Theses Room Collection of more than 1,500 AIS
MBA and MIT theses, dissertations and reports; a Special Collection, Main
Collection, Short Loan Collection and Reference Collection; photocopying,
printing and scanning; a self-checkout station; computers with access to
Microsoft Office, SPSS and MYOB; Wi-Fi; study rooms; a Collaborative Learning
Space; and individual consultation and tutorial rooms.

## Research databases

The Library provides access to a range of databases relevant to Information
Technology research, including IEEE Xplore (full-text electrical engineering,
electronics and computer science publications), Elsevier/ScienceDirect,
ProQuest Central, the Springer Nature Computer Science eBooks Collection,
Taylor & Francis eBooks in Computer Science, Gale OneFile Computer Science,
and the Dimensions database. Business-focused databases such as Business
One ProQuest and NZX Company Research are also available, along with
multidisciplinary collections.

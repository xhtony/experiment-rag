# MIT Entry Requirements, Pathways and Fees (2026)

## English language entry requirements

Entry to the Master of Information Technology requires one of the following
English language qualifications: IELTS 6.5 academic (with no band lower than
6.0); TOEFL iBT 79 (with a writing score of 21); PToE academic score of 58
with no band lower than 50; or NZCEL (Academic) Level 5.

## Academic entry requirements

In addition to the English language requirement, entry to the MIT requires a
bachelor degree, graduate diploma or postgraduate diploma, or equivalent, in
information technology, computer science or a related field of study.
Applications outside the stated criteria are assessed on a case-by-case basis.

## Specialist topics

MIT students choose one specialist topic. Software Development includes
SOFT806 Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment, SOFT807 Cloud
Application Development, and SOFT808 Software User Experience. Networks and
Security includes CONE809 Topics in Cloud Computing, CONE810 Computer and
Communication Network Security, and CONE811 Penetration Testing. Information
Systems includes INFO812 Data Mining, INFO813 Artificial Intelligence, and
INFO814 Enterprise Cloud-based Systems. Availability of the Networks and
Security and Information Systems topics should be verified before enrolment.

## Internship and project pathways

In the internship pathway, COMP803 Internship is completed within two
trimesters, or alternatively COMP806 Intensive Internship is completed within
one trimester. An industrial project pathway is also available. For the final
Level 9 component, the research option is COMP901 Information Technology
Research Thesis, and the applied option is COMP902 Advanced Information
Technology Specialised Project together with COMP903 Information Technology
Applied Research Project.

## Duration

The Master of Information Technology is a 16-month full-time programme with
three trimester intakes per year.

## Fees 2026 - international students

For international students, the 2026 programme fees are NZ$38,940 plus
resource fees of NZ$3,540, a total of NZ$42,480, plus a compulsory Student
Services Fee of NZ$300 per year (or NZ$30 per month or part thereof).

## Fees 2026 - domestic students

For domestic students, the 2026 programme fees are NZ$9,720 plus resource fees
of NZ$3,540, a total of NZ$13,260, plus a compulsory Student Services Fee of
NZ$300 per year (or NZ$30 per month or part thereof). The programme is
eligible for a student loan from StudyLink.

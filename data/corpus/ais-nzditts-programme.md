# New Zealand Diploma in Information Technology Technical Support (NZDITTS) Programme

## Programme details

The New Zealand Diploma in Information Technology Technical Support (NZDITTS)
at Auckland Institute of Studies (AIS) is a Level 5 qualification of 120
credits, delivered over 12 months (three trimesters). No previous tertiary
qualifications are necessary, and the diploma allows students to progress onto
further IT qualifications.

## Pathways after completion

Once the NZDIT Technical Support is completed, students can move onto a
Bachelor of Information Technology, followed by a Postgraduate Diploma or even
Master's study. Cross-credits for subsequent IT study are available.

## Programme structure

The qualification consists of 120 credits. There are eight courses worth 15
credits each, taken over three trimesters: COMP510 Fundamentals of Information
Systems and Technology, COMP511 Information Technology Professional Skills,
COMP513 Fundamentals of Computer Programming, COMP514 Fundamentals of Computer
Databases, COMP515 Fundamentals of Computer Networking, COMP516 Fundamentals
of Network Security, COMP517 Hardware and Software Service Provisioning, and
COMP518 Information Technology Systems Maintenance and Service Management.

## Entry requirements

Entry requires 40 credits at NCEA Level 2 or above, including at least 10
literacy credits and 10 numeracy credits, or equivalent. Applicants also
require an English level equivalent to IELTS 5.5 (Academic) with no band less
than 5.0 / TOEFL iBT 46 (with a writing score of 14) / PTE academic score of
42 with no band lower than 36 / AIS Test of English Proficiency (TEP) 48/80
(with no band less than 11).

## Fees

For international students, the 2025 programme fees are NZ$21,304 per 120
credits plus resource fees of NZ$2,280, a total of NZ$23,584. For domestic
students, the 2025 programme fees are NZ$4,216 per 120 credits plus resource
fees of NZ$2,280, a total of NZ$6,496. Both are subject to the compulsory
Student Services Fee of NZ$300 per year (or NZ$30 per month or part thereof).

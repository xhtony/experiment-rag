# Privacy, Enrolment Data and Insurance Requirements

## Privacy Act 2020 authorisation

In signing the enrolment form, students authorise any person or company to
provide to AIS such information as required in response to credit and
enrolment enquiries. Students further authorise AIS to furnish to any
governmental organisation or agency and to any other third party, including
parents or legal guardians of students under 18, details of the enrolment
application and subsequent dealings, including attendance and academic
records. AIS observes the general conditions governing the release of
information as set out in the Privacy Act 2020.

## Data storage and the National Student Index

Student names, dates of birth and residency information are included in the
National Student Index, and are used in authorised information matching
programmes with official government information such as the New Zealand Birth
Register, to verify the information provided. Data collected from tertiary
education organisations is stored in the Cloud: student enrolment and course
and qualification completion data is stored in a Microsoft datastore based in
Sydney, Australia.

## Access and correction of personal information

Students may see any information held about them and amend any errors in that
information by contacting the Academic Registrar.

## Medical and travel insurance requirement

International students must have approved travel and medical insurance cover
while studying in New Zealand and until the student visa expiry date.
Travel/medical insurance is compulsory for international students.

## Programme delivery conditions

The offering of programmes and courses is subject to minimum enrolment
numbers. AIS reserves the right to cancel or postpone a programme or course if
required.

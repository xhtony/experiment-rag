# Scholarship Options for Domestic Students

AIS offers a range of scholarships to domestic students. Students who are not
eligible for a scholarship may still be able to study fees free if they meet
the criteria for the government's fees-free programme.

## AIS Scholarship

Recipients of the AIS Scholarship award have 50% of the tuition fee paid for
the first year of study in any Diploma, Bachelor degree, Graduate Diploma,
Postgraduate Diploma or Master programme, including the MBA and MIT. The
scholarships are available to domestic students and are awarded on a
combination of academic merit and financial need.

## AIS Scholarship - how to apply

Applications for the AIS Scholarship must be emailed to scholarships@ais.ac.nz
and include: the applicant's academic record (including study completed
outside AIS); and a written statement explaining why the applicant is seeking
a scholarship, including full name, contact phone number, email address and
intended programme of study. Applications close four weeks before the start of
the intended semester. Successful applicants are notified two weeks before the
start of the semester. Applications received after the advertised closing date
are not considered.

## Women in Information Technology Scholarships

Recipients of the Women in Information Technology Scholarship award have 50%
of the tuition fee paid for the first year of study in any of the AIS
Information Technology programmes, including the Master of Information
Technology (MIT). These scholarships can save recipients more than $8,500.
Applicants email their application to scholarships@ais.ac.nz.

## Chairman's Scholarships

Five Chairman's Scholarships are available to domestic students. Chairman's
Scholarships provide eligible students with their fees paid in full for a
three-year bachelor degree in Hospitality Management, Information Technology,
Business or Tourism Management, worth up to $17,520. To be eligible, students
must: be a citizen or permanent resident of New Zealand, the Cook Islands,
Tokelau, Niue or Australia; be nominated by a secondary school; and be aged 17
or over. Students must also meet the entry requirements of the level at which
they are nominated, and demonstrate school and community participation such as
cultural participation, sports participation, student leadership (for example
peer mentoring) or community service. References from both school and
community (such as a church, sports club or other community group nominated by
the candidate's secondary school adviser or representative) are required.

## Other options

Other scholarship and funding options listed for domestic students include
Maori Grants, a Top Up Scholarship, the GOPIO West Auckland Scholarship, and
student loans.

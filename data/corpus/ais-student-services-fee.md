# Student Services Fee

## Fee rate

The Student Services Fee (SSF) covers the provision of a broad range of campus
services to students. The SSF is charged at a rate of NZ$300.00 per year, or
NZ$30.00 per month or part thereof. In 2023, the SSF covered 64% of the direct
costs of these services, with the remaining 36% being subsidised by AIS.

## Allocation of the fee

The fee is allocated to the following services, in consultation with students
via the Student/Management Forum: 52% to counselling services and pastoral
care (student orientation, student support and employee assistance programme);
18% to employment and careers information, advice and guidance; 20% to sports,
recreation and cultural activities (gym, sports teams and cultural events);
6% to students' associations and clubs (Student Council, student forums,
alumni and whanau events); 2% to financial support and advice; and 2% to
health services.

## Student/Management Forum

The Student/Management Forum, comprising student representatives from each
programme, meets once a trimester to provide input on non-academic matters,
including consultation on the Student Services Fee. Students can register
interest in attending the forum, or provide input, through the Student Support
Manager.

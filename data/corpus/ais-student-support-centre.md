# Student Support Centre (SSC)

## What the SSC does

The function of the AIS Student Support Centre (SSC) is to support and assist
students with gaining part-time or full-time employment by: helping students
prepare and write their CV (resume) and cover letter; advising on where and
how to search for work opportunities including paid employment, internships
and volunteer work; providing advice on interview preparation and techniques;
conducting employability workshops and industry workshops; and running a
careers expo.

## Location and opening hours

The AIS Student Support Centre is located at the St Helens Campus in room
M119. Students can phone or email to make an appointment, or come in during
opening hours. The Student Support opening hours are Monday to Friday, 8.30am
to 5.00pm. Enquiries can be emailed to studentcareers@ais.ac.nz.

## Student Job Search

Auckland Institute of Studies is an Associate Member of Student Job Search
(SJS). For AIS students, SJS is a free service aimed at assisting students in
their search for employment, from one-off jobs around the home through to
full-time graduate positions. Students can visit sjs.co.nz and register as a
student of Auckland Institute of Studies, then apply for advertised positions.
SJS can also be contacted on 0800 757 562 or student@sjs.co.nz.

# Student Visas, Attendance and Progress Requirements

## Visa requirement

New international students must receive an offer of place at AIS before they
can apply for a student visa, and the visa must remain current in order to
remain studying in New Zealand.

## Visa renewal requirements - attendance and pass rate

International students must pass at least 75% of their courses, and achieve
over 95% attendance, before Immigration New Zealand will accept a student visa
renewal application via Provider Direct Student (PDS).

## AIS Online Visa Office services

AIS offers an online visa service in partnership with Immigration New Zealand
for new and continuing students. The AIS Online Visa Office at the St Helens
Campus is open Monday to Friday between 10.00am and 3.00pm (except on public
holidays), and can assist with the My eQuals Platform, renew a current student
visa, and change a student visa from another provider to AIS.

## Applications the Online Visa Office cannot process

The AIS Online Visa Office cannot process renewals of expired visas of any
type, limited purpose visas, visa variations for a change of programme upon
arrival, or a change from a visitor or work visa to a student visa. For these,
manual applications must be made directly to Immigration New Zealand.

## Work visas for spouses and partners

If a student's spouse or partner has joined them in New Zealand, the spouse or
partner may be eligible for a New Zealand work visa under Immigration New
Zealand's Family Stream. To qualify, the student must be enrolled in a Level 7
(or higher) qualification (that is, a bachelor's degree or graduate diploma
programme) in an area of absolute skill shortage as specified in the Long Term
Skill Shortage List, or the Master of Business Administration (MBA). A work
visa application for the spouse or partner must be made directly to
Immigration New Zealand.

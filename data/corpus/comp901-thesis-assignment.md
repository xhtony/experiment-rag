# COMP901 IT Research Thesis - Assignment Brief

## Assessment overview

COMP901 Information Technology Research Thesis, Trimester 2 and 3, 2026. The
thesis is due at 5 p.m. on Monday 30 November 2026. This assignment is worth
80% of the total marks in the course, and there is a total of 100 marks in
this assignment.

## Learning outcomes

The assignment covers the following learning outcomes as listed in the Course
Outline: 1. Define a research problem, formulate research questions and
hypotheses, design and justify an appropriate methodology which addresses the
research questions and hypotheses. 2. Critically review relevant literature.
3. Conceptualise and develop a coherent programme of supervised research.
4. Independently execute a research plan to gather, verify and validate data.
5. Critically analyse and report progress and results.

## Submission requirements

Submission is via Moodle. If unable to submit via Moodle, email the submission
to your supervisor. Only electronic submissions will be marked; hard-copy
submissions will not be marked. All submissions are checked for plagiarism
using Turnitin.

## Thesis deliverable and word count

The thesis is the final and major deliverable of the research. It is a
complete volume that reports on the nature and outcomes of the research. It
sets out the research background, research questions and hypotheses,
experimental methodology, their results and analysis, and the conclusions
reached. It is a coherent report: it should not refer to or rely on other
deliverables from the course. It is expected to be between 30,000 and 50,000
words.

## Examination and final mark

The thesis will be marked by the student's supervisor, by an internal examiner
other than the supervisor, and by an examiner who is external to AIS. The
final mark for the thesis is an average of these three marks.

## Cover page and referencing requirements

Include a cover page that includes your name, student ID, course code and
title, and assignment title. All sources should be acknowledged in the main
body of the assignment and in a reference list at the end. Any
tools/software/applications used are also required to be referenced. Refer to
the 7th edition of the APA referencing style guide.

## Use of artificial intelligence tools

Any use of software, including artificial intelligence tools, should be
appropriately attributed, and a declaration detailing how AI was used
(including screenshots of prompts) should be included as an appendix. While AI
may be used as a tool for idea generation, it should be used with integrity as
with any other source. Upon submission, the assignment is checked for copied
materials as well as the use of AI, with penalties applied where appropriate
and if not referenced.

## Drafts and original work

The assignment should be the student's own work to demonstrate their thinking.
Any direct quotes from any source should be placed in quotation marks.
Students may resubmit draft versions of the assignment until the due date; on
the due date, the latest version will be accepted as the final version.
Similarity reports may take 24 hours to generate.

## Late submission penalty

Late submissions will be penalised by 5% of the available marks from the marks
awarded per day (24 hours) for up to four days, and receive a zero mark after
four days (96 hours).

## Thesis structure - front matter

The front matter should contain the following items, as appropriate: title
page, abstract, acknowledgements, table of contents, list of tables, and list
of figures.

## Thesis structure - body

The body should contain the following items, as appropriate: introduction;
critical review of the literature; statement of the research problem;
statement of the final research questions; statement of the final research
hypotheses; final scope of the research project; description of the research
methodology used; analysis of the data gained from the research; discussion of
the results, relating the results to the research questions and hypotheses,
including identification of any shortcomings or limitations in the research or
data; and conclusions and identification of potential future work.

## Thesis structure - end matter

The end-matter should include references, and appendices if needed.

## Marking scheme - presentation and research framing

The dissertation is worth 100 marks, distributed as follows. 1. The specified
front matter is present and correct (5 marks). 2. The English used is of a
professional standard, without spelling errors or significant grammatical
issues (10 marks). 3. The introduction clearly lays out the research
background and motivation (5 marks). 4. The literature review meets the
requirements of a professional literature review (10 marks). 5. The research
questions and hypotheses are clearly stated, and appropriate (5 marks). 6. The
scope is clearly stated, reasonable, and appropriate for a Master's thesis
(5 marks).

## Marking scheme - methodology, analysis and reporting

7. The description of the research methodology used is clear and reproducible
from the description (10 marks). 8. Analysis of the data is clearly reported,
and used appropriate techniques (15 marks). 9. Discussion is clear, relevant,
and identifies limitations (15 marks). 10. The conclusions are concise, clear,
relevant to the body of the thesis, and supported by evidence within the body
of the thesis (10 marks). 11. References are present, properly formatted, and
relevant (5 marks). 12. Any other end-matter is appropriate and relevant
(5 marks).

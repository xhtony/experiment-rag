# COMP901 IT Research Thesis - Marking Guide

## Marking guide overview

The COMP901 thesis marking guide applies to the Information Technology
Research Thesis due at 5 p.m. on Monday 30 November 2026. The dissertation is
worth 100 marks for this assessment and the assignment is worth 80% of the
course marks. Markers refer to the thesis marking sheet for the rubric; this
guide specifies the deduction rules applied under each marking criterion.

## Deductions - front matter and English

Criterion 1, specified front matter present and correct (5 marks): marks are
deducted for missing items or items with errors. Criterion 2, professional
standard of English (10 marks): marks are deducted for grammatical or spelling
errors.

## Deductions - introduction and literature review

Criterion 3, introduction (5 marks): marks are deducted for lack of clarity or
detail; the research background and motivation should be clearly laid out.
Criterion 4, literature review (10 marks): marks are deducted for poor
grouping of references and for irrelevant citations.

## Deductions - research questions and scope

Criterion 5, research questions and hypotheses (5 marks): marks are deducted
for lack of clarity, and if the research questions and hypotheses do not
relate. Criterion 6, scope (5 marks): marks are deducted for lack of clarity,
and if the scope is unreasonable.

## Deductions - methodology and data analysis

Criterion 7, research methodology (10 marks): marks are deducted for lack of
clarity, and if the methodology is unclear or inappropriate. Criterion 8, data
analysis (15 marks): marks are deducted for lack of clarity, and if
inappropriate analysis techniques are used.

## Deductions - discussion and conclusions

Criterion 9, discussion (15 marks): marks are deducted for lack of clarity,
for lack of relevance to the body or empirical work, and if limitations are
not present or not accurate. Criterion 10, conclusions (10 marks): marks are
deducted for lack of clarity and for irrelevant conclusions or material.

## Deductions - references and end-matter

Criterion 11, references (5 marks): marks are deducted for missing references,
for references that are not cited, and for improperly formatted references.
Criterion 12, other end-matter (5 marks): marks are deducted for unnecessary
or irrelevant end-matter.

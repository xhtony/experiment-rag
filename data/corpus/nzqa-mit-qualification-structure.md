# Master of Information Technology Qualification Structure (NZQA)

## Qualification requirements

A candidate must complete the requirements for one (or more) specialist topics
to be awarded the Master of Information Technology. The qualification is at
Level 9 with a total of 180 credits. The Master's degree can be taken as
Master's by coursework or Master's by research.

## Level 8 component - compulsory course

The programme of study shall comprise 90 credits of Level 8 Information
Technology courses, including one compulsory course: COMP801 Research Methods
(15 credits).

## Level 8 component - internship and project options

In addition to COMP801, candidates complete either: (b) one of COMP803
Internship (45 credits), COMP806 Intensive Internship (45 credits), COMP804
Industrial Project (45 credits), or COMP807 Intensive Industrial Project
(45 credits), plus 30 credits of specialist courses; or (c) COMP802 Research
Project (30 credits) plus 45 credits of specialist courses.

## Level 9 component - thesis or project pathway

The Level 8 component is followed by 90 credits of Level 9 Information
Technology courses, being either: (d) COMP901 Information Technology Research
Thesis (90 credits); or (e) COMP902 Advanced Information Technology
Specialised Project (45 credits) and COMP903 Information Technology Applied
Research Project (45 credits).

## Specialist courses - Software Development

Specialist courses (15 credits each) for the Master of Information Technology,
Software Development, are: SOFT806 Continuous Integration and Continuous
Deployment, SOFT807 Cloud Application Development, and SOFT808 Software User
Experience.

## Specialist courses - Networks and Security

Specialist courses (15 credits each) for the Master of Information Technology,
Networks and Security, are: CONE809 Topics in Cloud Computing, CONE810
Computer and Communication Network Security, and CONE811 Penetration Testing.

## Specialist courses - Information Systems

Specialist courses (15 credits each) for the Master of Information Technology,
Information Systems, are: INFO812 Data Mining, INFO813 Artificial
Intelligence, and INFO814 Enterprise Cloud-based Systems.

## Specialisation project substitution

Course COMP805 Specialisation Project may be substituted for any specialist
course from any specialist topic, with the approval of the Head of Programme.
